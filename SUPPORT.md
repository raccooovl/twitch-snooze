# Twitch Snooze support

Start with the [setup and troubleshooting guide](https://perfsn-extensions.basstank2004.chatgpt.site/twitch-snooze/guide/).

## Report a problem

[Open a bug report](https://github.com/raccooovl/twitch-snooze/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml) or email [support@perfsn.xyz](mailto:support@perfsn.xyz). Include:

- Your browser and extension version.
- The relevant Twitch page URL.
- What you expected, what happened, and how to reproduce it.
- The channel, category and whitelist status for filtering problems.

The browser's Extensions page shows your installed version. Store releases may differ while an update is under review. Refresh tabs that were already open after installing or updating.

GitHub issues are public. For screenshots, crop unrelated account details. Do not include passwords or cookies.

## Suggest an improvement

[Describe the problem](https://github.com/raccooovl/twitch-snooze/issues/new?template=feature-request.yml) you want solved and a concrete example of when it happens.

## Install and update

Use the official browser-store links on the [product page](https://perfsn-extensions.basstank2004.chatgpt.site/twitch-snooze/). The stores deliver updates automatically; the public source remains available for development.
